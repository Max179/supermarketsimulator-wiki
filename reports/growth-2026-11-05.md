## Pass 1 — reorder quantity

**Cluster: reorder quantity (进货补货 / 进货定价).** One new indexable guide:

- `/articles/reordering-by-what-is-on-the-shelf` — "Reordering by what is on the shelf, not by habit".
  12 steps, 5 common mistakes. The guide decides a reorder from the shelf the shop actually has: shelf stock
  versus incoming stock, rack slots, a box already sitting on a slot, the order notebook, the order screen's
  quantity controls, the market cost against the display price, the margin on the pricing screen, a bulk or
  wholesale saving only when shelf and back-room space can absorb it, box merging and storage sections, box
  placement areas, the restock loop, and a product's required shop level. Every step is grounded in facts already
  published on the entry pages; no number is estimated.

This is deliberately not another customer-route or profit guide. The existing reorder material covered what the
buying list is, how an offer works, and how to carry a crate; the gap was the quantity decision between a shelf
count and a confirmed order.

## Pass 2 — purchase-to-price bridge

**Cluster: purchase pricing (进货定价).** One new indexable guide:

- `/articles/pricing-a-new-product-before-the-first-sale` — "Pricing a new product before the first sale".
  10 steps, 6 common mistakes. The guide is the missing link between the buying list and the pricing screen: confirm
  the product in the catalogue, check the shop level it asks for, read its market cost on the day, set the first
  price on the pricing canvas, test the number against the computed margin, use a quick pick only as a shortcut,
  check whether the product prices itself, keep offers and discounts out of the shelf price, check the rack or
  display slot before the first box is shelved, and judge the result from the day's own record before repricing.
  It is deliberately not a customer-route, daily-profit, one-pass-repricing or reorder-quantity guide; it answers
  the earlier question those guides leave open: what should this new line sell for in the first place?
