# Image replacement — supermarketsimulator.wiki, 2026-10-30

**Why.** The build shipped 164 raw Unity texture exports as the pictures on entry pages: UI atlases, white-on-black
icon sheets, grey placeholders and ground textures. They read as broken pictures rather than pictures of the game. The
Lead built a sourcing pipeline for in-game pictures (official Steam store screenshots and files from the game's
community wiki on Fandom) and delivered two frozen packs; this record covers both revisions and ends at the tree now
committed.

## Revision 1 — first pack (commit `c0bc125`)

- `content/assets/mapped/`: 164 raw texture exports deleted, 100 normalised in-game pictures (jpg, 1200x750) added;
  94 original diagrams untouched. `content/images-manifest.json` replaced with 100 records carrying `sourceName`,
  `sourcePage`, `originalUrl`, `license`, `rightsReview`, `retrievedAt`, `verdict`, `reviewer`, `reviewDate` and
  sha256/bytes/dimensions. 100 published entries updated (new picture first, original diagrams kept); the 19
  diagram-only entries untouched. Home page hero/featured replaced with `web/assets/hero-shop.jpg` and
  `web/assets/featured-card.jpg`.
- Source mix: 61 Fandom wiki files + 39 official Steam store screenshots; 48 unique pictures.
- Review found three pack defects and reported them: 49 entries whose picture clearly showed a different subject
  (item renders such as book covers, coins and beer bottles attached to system entries), 39 Steam records whose
  `sourcePage` was a third-party article rather than the store page, and a wiki FAQ-banner picture with a large text
  overlay used on four entries.

## Revision 2 — swap pack (this tree)

- The complete mapped set was replaced: 100 pictures, 67 of them byte-different from revision 1. The manifest and the
  100 pictured entry files were replaced from the swap pack (67 entries differ; 33 are reprints; the 19 diagram-only
  entries stay as they were). Source list and review ledger regenerated by the pack.
- Fixed: every one of the 49 Steam records now has `sourcePage = https://store.steampowered.com/app/2670630/` with
  `originalUrl` kept as the CDN link; the FAQ-banner picture is gone from every entry; no selected picture is under
  600 px wide (all 100 are 1200x750); 42 of the 49 previously wrong-subject entries were remapped; reuse notes now read
  "Shared with N other entrie(s) as a category-level in-game picture."; the sources-page home-page credit now links the
  official store page (`PACK.json hero.source`).
- Source mix now: 51 Fandom wiki files + 49 official Steam store screenshots (the pack's own taxonomy is 15 wiki
  files + 49 official store screenshots + 36 wiki screenshots = 100). 29 unique pictures; 11 shared groups covering
  82 records (largest group 14, then 9); every record is `fair_use_review`; the review ledger has a verdict row for
  all 100 files.
