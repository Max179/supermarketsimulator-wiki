# Image sanity check

Generated by tools/image-sanity.py from content/images-manifest.json and the picture files on site.
Transparent pictures are composited on the dark page background before measuring, because that is how the
pages draw them. A picture is flagged when it carries almost no variation (stddev below 12), when more
than 90% of its pixels sit within 6 of the median, or when the median is near-black (below 24) or
near-white (above 235). A flag is not a verdict: it means the file has to be looked at by eye.
